# 1. Time Value of Money

The time value of money (TVM) equation is:

$$\text{FV} = {\rm PV}\left( 1 + \frac{i}{n} \right)^{n\cdot t}$$
^tvm

This relates the future value $\rm FV$ of some amount of cash now, called present value $\rm PV$, by an interest rate $i$ over $t$ years with $n$ compounding periods per year.

- $\rm FV \to$ Future Value
- $\rm PV \to$ Present Value
- $t \to$ Number of years
- $n \to$ Number of compounding periods per year
- $i \to$ Interest Rate

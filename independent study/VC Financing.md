# VC Financing

## SAFE

Simple Agreement for Future Equity

Lent an amount of money debt free on the condition that the investment will be converted onto shares at the next fundraising round.

### SAFE with Valuation Cap

Valuation Cap is a cap on the valuation of the company when converting the investment into shares at the later round. This means the shares are discounted.

Let
- $P$ be the amount given by the SAFE
- $V_{\rm cap}$ is the agreed valuation cap
- $N$ is the total number of shares issued (in the future)
- $V'$ is the valuation of the company after(?) the next round of fundraising.
  
If we issue $N=1 \rm M$ shares, and sell some number of shares at $\$100 / \text{share}$, meaning the valuation of the company is $\$100/\text{share} \times 1 \text{M shares}=\$100 \text{M}=V'$. 

Now, the SAFE matures and the VC's, initial investment, say $P=\$1 \rm M$, is converted into shares *at the discounted rate*. Say it was agreed that $V_{\rm cap}= \$ 10 \rm M$; this means that shares are bought *as if* the company was still worth $\$10 \rm M$. 

- $S$; True share price $S=\$100/\text{share}$
- $S'$: Reduced share price: $V_cap = N \times S' \Rightarrow \$10 \rm M = \text{1M Shares} \times \underline{\text{\$10/share}}$

Which means they get a $10\times$ ROI. The equation for times ROI is:

$$\text{ROI}_{\%} = \frac{V'}{V_{\rm cap}}$$

And their dollars ROI is:

$$\text{ROI}_{\$} = P \cdot \text{ROI}_{\%}$$

The percentage of the company they own $F_{\rm vc}$ is:

$$F_{\rm vc} = \frac{\text{ROI}_{\$}}{V'} = \frac{\displaystyle P\left(\frac{V'}{V_{\rm cap}}\right)}{V'} = \frac{P}{V_{\rm cap}}$$
In this case,

$$F_{\rm vc} = \frac{\text{\$1 M}}{\text{\$10 M}} = 10 \%$$
Which makes sense because their $\text{ROI}_{\$} = \text{\$10 M}$ and the company itself is worth $\text{\$100 M}$.

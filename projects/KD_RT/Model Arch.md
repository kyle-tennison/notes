# Model Arch

Symbols
- $\hat{\bf y'}$  Normalized predicted mean
- $\hat{\bf y}$ Normalized true mean
- $\mathbf y'$ Predicted mean
- $\mathbf y$ True mean
- $\mathbf x$ Feature maps


Loss functions:
- Spatial model loss:

$$\text{loss}=\text{mean}\left( (\hat{\mathbf y} - \hat{\mathbf y'} )^2 \right)$$

# 1. Premature De-Industrialization

There are three primary sectors:
1. Agriculture
2. Industry
3. Services

Services break down into two categories:
- Tradable services:
	- E.g. financial services, consulting, etc. 
	- Can be imported or exported
	- High skill necessary
	- Employ fewer, higher productivity, higher wages
- Non-tradable services:
	- E.g. personal services, hospitality, etc.
	- Cannot be imported/exported
	- Less skill necessary
	- Employ a lot, mid productivity, mid/low wages

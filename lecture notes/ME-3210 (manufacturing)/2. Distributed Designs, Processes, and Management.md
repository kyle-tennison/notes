Assembly Index (Design Efficiency)

$$E_m = \frac{({\rm NM})t_a}{\rm TM}$$

where:
- $\rm NM$ is the minimum number of parts
- $t_a$ is the theoretical lowest assembly time for one part, =3 sec (see (14.1) of Kalpakjian’s)
- $\rm TM$ is the total assembly time
